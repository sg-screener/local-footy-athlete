## THE ONE QUESTION THAT DECIDES THE REST

**Does accessory work INSIDE a strength session count toward the week's accessory
volume?**

Everything below changes depending on your answer, so it is first.

The Bible says accessories and prehab live inside sessions:

> `:224` — "Canonical order inside a session: power -> main -> secondary ->
> accessories -> finisher. **Midline and prehab sit after the accessories.**"

> `:229` — "One optional 'Mobility & Prehab flow' sits at the TOP of the session,
> collapsed… **The flow is NEVER load-bearing: the app assumes athletes skip it,
> so any prehab that actually matters lives in the session itself, not in the
> flow.**"

So a normal lower day already carries prehab and midline rows after its
accessories, and `:229` says that is where the prehab that MATTERS lives.

- **If in-session rows COUNT:** the top-up fires rarely — only when the week's
  sessions genuinely left a region untouched. That reads like your "final 10%".
- **If they DON'T count:** the top-up fires most weeks, because a standalone
  accessories session is then the only thing that satisfies it. That reads like a
  default wearing a need test, which is what you just killed.

**I recommend: in-session rows COUNT.** It is what `:229` says, and it is the
only reading under which "if anything is lacking" is a real test rather than a
formality. **But it is your call and I have not assumed it** — every threshold
below is written as though they count, and each is marked so you can see what
moves if they do not.

## §1 — WHAT IS MEASURED

Three volumes. Nothing else is measured, because nothing else has a Bible line.

### V1 — Accessory region coverage

**What:** which prehab regions the week touched, counted from every row in the
week carrying `role: 'strength_accessory'`, wherever it sits — inside a strength
session or in a standalone Accessories session.

**Regions** (your signed six pools): groin/adductors · calves · lower prehab ·
midline (trunk anti-rotation) · shoulder health · hamstring.

**Bible:** `:152` authorises the SESSION at four in-season days —
> "4 days: as above plus **optional accessories/prehab Wednesday** and an
> optional Gunshow Friday."

**Bible does NOT say:** how many regions a week should touch, or that regions are
the right unit at all. *Alternatives I considered and rejected: counting SETS
(the Bible never doses accessories weekly) and counting SESSIONS (which cannot
see that a week did plenty of shoulder work and no groin work — the exact lack
your example names).*

### V2 — Gunshow

**What:** count of gunshow sessions in the week.

**Bible:** `:153` — "Everyone may take an optional Gunshow at G-1." `:81` names a
Friday gunshow in all three ideal weekly structures.

**Not need-based.** R1 is authored and fixture-relative. V2 is measured only so
the top-up never adds a second gunshow on top of the authored one.

### V3 — Mobility coverage

**What:** count of mobility sessions in the week.

**Bible, off-season:** `:108` — "3-4 strengths, 3-5 conditioning and **1-2
mobility** is probably a decent starting point."

**Bible, in-season and pre-season:** `:104` — mobility gains belong to the
off-season, and
> "Trying to increase mobility during the season or even in pre season when you
> have games and demands of changing direction… **then you risk injury**"

**So mobility has an authored target in ONE phase only.** In-season and
pre-season it stays athlete-add plus the in-session flow. I have written no
in-season mobility need, and I think writing one would contradict `:104`.

## §4 — WHAT THE PASS DOES NOT DO

Stated because a top-up pass is the kind of thing that grows.

- **It never adds strength or conditioning.** Those are the 90% and the contract
  owns them. If the week is short on either, that is a §18 shortfall and its own
  repair — not a top-up.
- **It never fills empty days.** R3 is killed. An empty day is only filled if a
  NEED names it, and a need places at most one session.
- **It never runs on the athlete's own additions.** If they added accessories,
  V1 sees those rows and the need does not fire. That is the intended behaviour
  and it follows from measuring rows rather than sessions.
- **It never places recovery.** No need measures recovery, and recovery has no
  authored composition.

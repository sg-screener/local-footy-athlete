## Monday 10 August

### Training Day

**Lower Body Strength**

- Back Squat — 3 × 2-4
  - Sit into the hips, chest up. Own the bottom position.
- Deadlift — 3 × 2-4
  - Keep bar close, get tight through the lats. Flat back from start to lockout.
- Walking Lunges — 3 × 6-8
  - Long stride, tall through the midline. Steady and deliberate.
- Single-Leg RDL — 2 × 6-8
  - Hinge at the hip, soft knee. Balance and control over speed.
- Band Pallof Press — 2 × 8-12
  - Press out, resist the pull. Hips stay square, slight knee and hip bend.

**Conditioning**

- Short Flush — 1 × 1  ⚠ **"1 × 1" IS NOT A PRESCRIPTION — how much of this, and how hard?**
  - In and done — should finish feeling better than you started, full stop. Very easy, 3–4/10 — finish better than you started

## Tuesday 11 August

### Training Day

**Upper Pull**

- Pull-Ups — 3 × 4-6
  - Dead hang to chin over bar, any grip is fine. Initiate with the lats, not the arms. Add weight once bodyweight sets feel easy.
- Barbell Row — 3 × 4-6
  - Hinge forward, pull to the belly. Squeeze the shoulder blades at the top.
- Face Pull — 2 × 10-20
  - Pull to the forehead, open the hands out. Squeeze the rear delts, slow return.

## Wednesday 12 August

### Training Day

**Accessories**

- Dragon Flag — 2 × 4-6
  - Control the way down, low back should be slightly rounded and roll over ground.
- Cossack Squat — 3 × 6-8
  - Wide stance, sit onto one leg, other leg straight. Heel down, chest up, push back through. Hold rack if needed.
- Bottoms-Up KB Press — 2 × 6-8
  - Bell vertical, wrist stacked. Press smooth, keep ribs down.
- Single-Leg Calf Raise — 3 × 12-15
  - Full drop at the bottom, pause, then rise. Three-second lowering.

## Thursday 13 August

### Training Day

**Upper Push**

- Overhead Press — 3 × 3-5
  - Ribs down, bar finishes over the ears. Press hard and finish strong.
- DB Bench Press — 3 × 8-15
  - Shoulder blades set on the bench. Control the dumbbells down and drive.
- Lateral Raise — 2 × 10-20
  - Light weight, lead with the elbows. No momentum, strict form.

## Friday 14 August

### Training Day

**Gunshow**

- Incline Dumbbell Curl — 3 × 10-12
  - Let the arm stretch at the bottom. Slow curl, squeeze at the top.
- Banded Bicep Curl — 3 × 15-20
  - Elbows pinned, curl against the band. Slow on the way down.
- Tricep Pushdown — 3 × 12-15
  - Keep elbows pinned, press to full lockout. Constant tension, no jerking.
- Overhead Tricep Extension — 2 × 10-12
  - Full stretch at the bottom, press to lockout. Elbows stay pointed forward.
- Rear Delt Fly — 3 × 12-15
  - Rest on incline bench, pinch the shoulder blades. Light weight, feel the squeeze.
- Shrugs — 3 × 10-12
  - Shoulders straight up to ears, no roll. Squeeze at the top, controlled lower.

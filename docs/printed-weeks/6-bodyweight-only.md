# In-season with nothing but a floor — no gym at all

**Week of Monday 10 August.**

**What to look for:** The athlete owns no equipment. Every exercise here has to be doable in a hotel room or a park. Is any of it impossible without kit, and is there enough of it to be worth opening the app for?

## Monday 10 August

### Training Day

**Strength**

- Vertical Jump — 2 × 3
  - Every rep fast and sharp. Land soft in athletic stance. Stop if reps get slow.
- Bodyweight Squat — 3 × 4
  - Sit back into hips, knees follow toes, chest tall.
- Glute Bridge — 2 × 15
  - Keep spine neutral, don’t arch low back, lift hips, squeeze glutes, add weight to hips if you can.
- Single-Leg RDL — 3 × 7
  - Hinge at the hip, soft knee. Balance and control over speed.
- Cossack Squat — 3 × 10
  - Wide stance, sit onto one leg, other leg straight. Heel down, chest up, push back through. Hold rack if needed.
- Bosch Hold — 2 × 10
  - Single or double leg, slight knee bend, drive heel into ground, keep hips high.

## Monday 10 August

### Training Day

**Lower Body Strength**

- Walking Lunges — 3 × 6-8
  - Long stride, tall through the midline. Steady and deliberate.
- Single-Leg RDL — 2 × 6-8
  - Hinge at the hip, soft knee. Balance and control over speed.
- Reverse Lunges — 3 × 6-8
  - Step back, drop straight down. Front knee tracks over the toe.
- Glute Bridge — 2 × 6-8
  - Keep spine neutral, don’t arch low back, lift hips, squeeze glutes, add weight to hips if you can.
- Band Pallof Press — 2 × 8-12  ⚠ **CANNOT BE DONE — needs bands, which this athlete does not have.**
  - Press out, resist the pull. Hips stay square, slight knee and hip bend.
- RDLs — 3 × 6-10  ⚠ **CANNOT BE DONE — needs barbell, which this athlete does not have.**
  - Push hips back, bar slides down leg. Feel the hamstrings load on the way down.

## Wednesday 12 August

### Training Day

**Accessories**

- Side Plank — 30-45s
  - Stack the hips, straight line from head to feet, can add weight to top hip. Breathe behind the brace.
- Cossack Squat — 3 × 6-8
  - Wide stance, sit onto one leg, other leg straight. Heel down, chest up, push back through. Hold rack if needed.
- Scap Push-Up — 2 × 10-15
  - Keep elbows locked, glide shoulder blades. Move slowly, no sagging hips.
- Single-Leg Calf Raise — 3 × 12-15
  - Full drop at the bottom, pause, then rise. Three-second lowering.

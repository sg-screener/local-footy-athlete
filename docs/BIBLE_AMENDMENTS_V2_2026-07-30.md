## 1. Optional work — REPLACES the withdrawn amendment 1

**The withdrawn draft said:** "the app plans REST and offers the recovery door;
it never places recovery uninvited", generalised toward a blanket ban.

**Proposed amendment (your law, 2026-07-30):**

> **THE OPTIONAL PLACEMENT LAW.** The app MAY place optional work — gunshow,
> accessories, mobility, optional conditioning, optional strength — only when all
> five hold:
>
> 1. a placement rule Sam has authored
> 2. composition Sam has authored
> 3. rendered visibly optional
> 4. binnable in one tap
> 5. never counted toward compliance, load, or rest
>
> Optional work that fails any of the five is not placed. Early off-season's
> all-optional contracts are the pattern the law is drawn from.

**What this permits that the withdrawn version forbade:** the G-1 gunshow,
G-3 accessories, the bye support slot, and every optional session an authored
contract calls for. `g_minus_1_optional_only` stands; the three ideal weekly
structures stand.

**What it still forbids, and why recovery is still not placed:** recovery failed
three of the five — no rule, invented composition, and it consumed the rest
quota.

**If you don't sign it:** the app has no stated basis for the optional placements
it already makes, and `docs/OPTIONAL_PLACEMENT_SHEET_2026-07-30.md` has eight
rules waiting on you, two of which cite no authority at all.

## 2. The rest quota — UNCHANGED from the first draft

**Proposed amendment:**

> **THE REST LAW.** The rest quota counts days on which nothing was REQUIRED of
> the athlete. Athlete-added optional work — recovery, mobility, prehab,
> gunshow — never breaks a rest day. A day may be both rested and active, and the
> week reports both.

**If you don't sign it:** the app goes back to charging athletes a rest day for
their own recovery, and `section18ContractV2Tests` 8a/8b/P5 revert with it.

## 4. Mobility — REPLACES the withdrawn amendment 4

**The withdrawn draft said** a flow is "drawn whole from the authored flows", and
asked you to rule on a 4-movement bundle.

**You do not recognise the bundles, and the trace agrees with you** — the
exercises are yours, the groupings came from one commit with no ruling cited
(`docs/OPTIONAL_PLACEMENT_LAW_SUPERSESSION_2026-07-30.md` §5). The floor question
is dissolved: there are no bundles left to be too short.

**Proposed amendment:**

> **MOBILITY.** A mobility session is COMPOSED from the mobility pool: 5-8
> movements at their authored warm-up doses, spread across lower, hips, midline
> and upper. No pre-built flow sits between the pool and the athlete. Counts
> toward nothing: never a hard day, no exposure credit, never breaks rest.

**Still needs you:** which region each of the twenty mobility exercises belongs
to. Proposed as a table in `OPTIONAL_PLACEMENT_SHEET_2026-07-30.md` §3 — it is
the only invention in the replacement, and it is not trusted until signed.

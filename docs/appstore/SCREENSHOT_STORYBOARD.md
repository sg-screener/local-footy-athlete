# App Store Screenshot Storyboard (drafted 2026-07-23)

Capture AFTER Groups C/D land (final copy + accessibility polish on screen).
Apple uses the FIRST THREE on install sheets — they carry the whole story.
Accepted sizes (from App Store Connect): 1242×2688, 2688×1242, 1284×2778 or
2778×1284. Simulator iPhone 17 Pro screenshots at native scale are fine.

## Capture setup

- Seeded, realistic in-season week (`standard-in-season-week` seed — real
  exercise names, a game on Saturday, team training days visible).
- Clean status bar (full battery, no debug banners; RN dev banner off).
- Light-touch framing: solid black background panel + one short yellow
  caption line above each shot, brand type. Build frames in Canva/Figma
  from the raw captures. Keep captions ≤6 words, benefit-first.
- No device bezels needed (Apple accepts raw screens; bezel frames are a
  style choice — if used, use them on all shots consistently).

## The six shots, in order

| # | Screen | Caption | Why this shot |
|---|--------|---------|---------------|
| 1 | Week view — full week visible: strength day, conditioning, team training marked, game day locked/badged | "Your season. Your program." | The hero. Proves it's a real, structured, footy-aware program in one glance. |
| 2 | Day/session view — exact exercises, sets × reps, target loads | "Know exactly what to do today." | Answers "is this a real program or fluff?" — the density/precision shot. |
| 3 | Plan-change in action — a swap/move applied with its plain-language disclosure visible ("…moved to Thursday. Undo anytime.") | "Life happens. Your week adapts." | The differentiator. No competitor shows honest, reversible adaptation. |
| 4 | Readiness — "I'm not 100%" card + lighter-day offer ("Make today lighter?") | "Train for how you feel." | The care/trust shot; mirrors the description's promise. |
| 5 | Coach chat — a deterministic exchange (e.g. swapping an exercise) with the coach's plain reply | "Ask the coach. Get answers." | Shows the coach without overpromising free-text editing. |
| 6 | Season setup — team training days / game day / equipment step | "Set up your season in minutes." | Reduces download friction: shows onboarding is quick and footy-specific. |

Optional 7th: the game-day locked view ("Game day stays protected.") if a
slot feels empty — it reinforces the protective-coach brand.

## Rules

- Every screenshot must show REAL app behavior (Apple checks; so do users).
  No mockups of unbuilt features. If a screen changes in C/D, recapture.
- Same seed across all shots so day names/sessions are coherent between
  screenshots (users notice).
- Captions must not claim preview-before-approve or free-text coach editing
  (v1 truth: disclose-after with undo; deterministic coach).

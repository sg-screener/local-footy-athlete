Local Footy Athlete ("LFA", "the app") is made by SR Digital Pty Ltd
("we", "us"), an Australian company. This policy explains what
information the app handles and what we do with it.

## The short version

Your training data stays on your phone. We don't run ads, we don't sell
data, and we don't track you across other apps. The only personal
information we receive is what you send us directly — like your email
address when you use the in-app feedback form.

## What stays on your device

Your program, workouts, logged weights, session feedback, readiness
reports, injuries, season settings and profile are stored locally on
your device. We do not store them on our servers. If you delete the
app, that data is deleted with it.

## What we process to run the app

Program generation and coach chat: when you set up your program or
message the coach, the app sends your message and the relevant parts of
your training setup (your week's sessions, season phase, equipment,
goals, and any injury settings you've entered) to our server, which
uses an AI provider (OpenAI) to generate your program or the coach's
reply. This information is used only to produce
that response. We don't store it, and it is not used for advertising or
to train AI models. No account or identity is attached to these
requests.

## What we receive and keep

Feedback: if you use "Leave Feedback" or "Ask a Human" in the app, we
receive the message you write and the email address you provide, so we
can read it and reply. We use it for nothing else.

## What we don't do

We don't show ads. We don't sell or share your personal information.
We don't use analytics or tracking SDKs. We don't track you across
apps or websites.

## Health disclaimer

LFA provides general strength and conditioning programming for
Australian rules football. It is not medical advice. Train within your
limits and consult a professional for injuries or medical conditions.

## Children

LFA is intended for users aged 13 and over.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the "last updated"
date. Material changes will be noted in the app's release notes.

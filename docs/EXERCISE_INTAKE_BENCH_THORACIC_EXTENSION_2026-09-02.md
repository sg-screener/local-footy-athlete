## 12. Bench Thoracic Extension

- **Video:** https://youtube.com/shorts/xE5ZaEKAx1g?si=Ylfp3Xa034vjwtZS
- **Category:** Mobility.
- **Main muscles:** Lats; upper back.
- **Secondary muscles:** Triceps; chest; midline.
- **Primary cue:** Place elbows on the bench and sit your hips back.
- **Secondary cue:** Sink your chest without arching through the lower back.
- **Equipment:** Bench OR plyo box.
- **Unilateral:** No; repetitions are total.
- **Experience:** Everyone.
- **Session use:** Upper-body warm-up; Mobility; Recovery; overhead preparation.
- **Availability:** Automatic in those uses; manual Add/Swap.
- **Season:** All phases.
- **Near-game suitability:** Good; suitable the day before a game at a comfortable range.
- **Prescription:** 2 × 5 slow reps; pause 3 seconds at the bottom; 30 seconds rest.
- **Loading:** Bodyweight only.
- **Loading demand:** Low.
- **Fatigue:** Low.
- **Soreness:** Low.
- **Balance/stabilisation demand:** Low.
- **Eccentric demand:** Low.

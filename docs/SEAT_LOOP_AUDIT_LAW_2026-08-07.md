## The law, binding on every seat (review seat AND terminal)

THIRD SIGHTING = MANDATORY AUDIT. When any of these hits three, the
seat stops and proposes a compression — it does not run the loop a
fourth time in silence:

1. Same ruling issued with only the nouns changed → propose a standing
   rule with explicit stop-conditions (precedent:
   docs/STANDING_DERIVATION_RULING_2026-08-07.md, which took NINE
   stops to exist and should have taken three).
2. A named defect class's sighting count rising (this repo already
   counts them — "sixth sighting of harness-enters-below-the-door";
   counting without structural response is the failure) → at every
   multiple of three, a compression proposal or a recorded reason why
   not.
3. The same round-trip, toll, manual correction, or rediscovery
   repeating → batch it, gate it, or write the artifact where the next
   session looks.

The audit answers five questions in plain language: what repeated,
what each repetition cost, what stayed invariant (that sentence IS the
standing rule), what still varies (those ARE the stop-conditions), and
the smallest structural change — then records the outcome in docs/ or
the handoff so it survives the session.

Guardrail: never compress a loop whose recent outcomes still CHANGE
(refuted designs are the loop earning its keep). Compress when the
outcomes stopped changing. Unrecoverable cases (decision payloads,
Sam-signed behaviour) always keep their stop.

Existing laws that were late compressions, listed as the evidence:
bible-first, the batch rule, RULE-DON'T-ASK, totals-or-red, the
printed-exit-line rule, the standing derivation ruling. Every one
existed in loop form long before it was named. The point of this law
is that the NEXT one gets named at three.

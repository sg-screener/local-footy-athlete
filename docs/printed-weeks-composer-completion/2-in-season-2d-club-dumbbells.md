## Tuesday 14 July

### Training Day

**Strength**

- Goblet Squat — 3 × 2-4
  - Elbows inside the knees at the bottom. Smooth tempo, no rushing.
- Single-Leg RDL — 3 × 6-8
  - Hinge at the hip, soft knee. Balance and control over speed.
- Single-Arm DB Floor Press — 3 × 3-5
  - Brace the midline, press from the floor. Don't let the torso rotate.
- Single-Arm DB Row — 3 × 4-6
  - Elbow drives past the torso, pull to belly. Keep the hips and shoulders square.
- Band Pallof Press — 2 × 10-15
  - Press out, resist the pull. Hips stay square, slight knee and hip bend.

## Thursday 16 July

### Training Day

**Strength**

- RDLs — 3 × 2-4
  - Push hips back, bar slides down leg. Feel the hamstrings load on the way down.
- Cossack Squat — 3 × 8-12
  - Wide stance, sit onto one leg, other leg straight. Heel down, chest up, push back through. Hold rack if needed.
- DB Shoulder Press — 3 × 3-5
  - Start at the shoulders, press to lockout. No arching through the lower back.
- Band Pull-Apart — 3 × 4-6
  - Squeeze the shoulder blades together. Elbows straight, controlled return.
- Banded Dead Bug — 2 × 10-15
  - Band behind head, pull band with straight arms toward knees, ribs down. Lower back stays glued to the floor.

## Friday 17 July

### Training Day

**Gunshow**

- Banded Bicep Curl — 3 × 15-20
  - Elbows pinned, curl against the band. Slow on the way down.
- Concentration Curl — 2 × 12-15
  - Elbow braced on the inner thigh. Strict curl, no body swing.
- Dumbbell Kickback — 2 × 12-15
  - One knee on bench, one hand on bench, extend to full lockout. Squeeze at the top, slow return.
- Banded Tricep Pushdown — 3 × 15-20
  - Lock the elbows, press to full extension. Constant tension, no slack.
- Single-Arm Shrug — 3 × 10-12
  - One side at a time, shoulder straight up. Pause at the top, controlled lower.
- Lateral Raise — 3 × 12-15
  - Light weight, lead with the elbows. No momentum, strict form.

## Things wrong with this week

## Tuesday 14 July

### Training Day

**Strength**

- Back Squat — 3 × 2-4
  - Sit into the hips, chest up. Own the bottom position.
- Single-Leg RDL — 3 × 6-8
  - Hinge at the hip, soft knee. Balance and control over speed.
- Bench Press — 3 × 3-5
  - Shoulder blades pinched, bend bar like snapping a twig, feet planted. Bar to chest, press hard to lockout.
- Pull-Ups — 3 × 4-6
  - Dead hang to chin over bar, any grip is fine. Initiate with the lats, not the arms. Add weight once bodyweight sets feel easy.
- Ab Wheel — 2 × 10-15
  - Roll out only as far as you can control. Ribs down, slightly rounded back, no sagging at hips.

### Training Day

**Strength**

- Deadlift — 3 × 2-4

  - Slight lean forward. Control the descent, no bouncing.
- Overhead Press — 3 × 3-5
  - Ribs down, bar finishes over the ears. Press hard and finish strong.

  - Hinge forward, pull to the belly. Squeeze the shoulder blades at the top.

## Friday 17 July

### Training Day

**Gunshow**

- Concentration Curl — 2 × 12-15
  - Elbow braced on the inner thigh. Strict curl, no body swing.
- Chin-Up Negative (Slow) — 2 × 4-6
  - Jump to the top, lower slowly, can pull yourself up if you can. Fight gravity the whole way.
- Dumbbell Kickback — 2 × 12-15
  - One knee on bench, one hand on bench, extend to full lockout. Squeeze at the top, slow return.
- Banded Tricep Pushdown — 3 × 15-20
  - Lock the elbows, press to full extension. Constant tension, no slack.
- Single-Arm Shrug — 3 × 10-12
  - One side at a time, shoulder straight up. Pause at the top, controlled lower.
- Lateral Raise — 3 × 12-15
  - Light weight, lead with the elbows. No momentum, strict form.

# Pre-season, five days, no club — full gym

**Week of Monday 13 July.**

**What to look for:** This week would not build at all until the full-body day was taught to ask what the rest of the week is missing. Look at the first day: it is the one carrying squat, deadlift, single-leg work, a press and a pull. Is that too much for one session?

---

## Monday 13 July

### Training Day

**Strength**

- Back Squat — 3 × 4-6
  - Sit into the hips, chest up. Own the bottom position.
- Deadlift — 3 × 4-6
  - Keep bar close, get tight through the lats. Flat back from start to lockout.
- Bulgarian Split Squats — 3 × 6-10
  - Slight lean forward. Control the descent, no bouncing.
- Single-Leg RDL — 3 × 6-10
  - Hinge at the hip, soft knee. Balance and control over speed.
- Bench Press — 3 × 4-6
  - Shoulder blades pinched, bend bar like snapping a twig, feet planted. Bar to chest, press hard to lockout.
- Barbell Row — 3 × 4-8
  - Hinge forward, pull to the belly. Squeeze the shoulder blades at the top.
- DB Shoulder Press — 2 × 12-15
  - Start at the shoulders, press to lockout. No arching through the lower back.

**Conditioning**

- Short Flush — 1 × 1  ⚠ **"1 × 1" IS NOT A PRESCRIPTION — how much of this, and how hard?**
  - In and done — should finish feeling better than you started, full stop. Very easy, 3–4/10 — finish better than you started

## Tuesday 14 July

### Training Day

**Strength**

- Front Squat — 3 × 4-6
  - Elbows high, sit between the heels. Stay tall through the midline.
- Hip Thrusts — 3 × 6-10
  - Drive through the heels, squeeze at the top. Ribs down, no overextending.
- Cossack Squat — 3 × 8-12
  - Wide stance, sit onto one leg, other leg straight. Heel down, chest up, push back through. Hold rack if needed.
- Single-Leg RDL — 3 × 6-10
  - Hinge at the hip, soft knee. Balance and control over speed.
- Ab Wheel — 2 × 8-12
  - Roll out only as far as you can control. Ribs down, slightly rounded back, no sagging at hips.

**Conditioning**

- 30:30 Controlled Tempo Blocks — 13 × 1
  - Controlled, not max — you should be able to hold a conversation in three words by the end, not one. 65–80% MAS — controlled, NOT max

## Wednesday 15 July

### Training Day

**Strength**

- Incline Bench — 3 × 4-6
  - Set the blades on the incline. Press through the palms.
- Seated DB Press — 3 × 8-10
  - Back flat against the pad, press to lockout. Ribs down, control the descent.
- Band Pull-Apart — 2 × 8-10
  - Squeeze the shoulder blades together. Elbows straight, controlled return.

**Conditioning**

- 30:30 Controlled Tempo Blocks — 13 × 1
  - Controlled, not max — you should be able to hold a conversation in three words by the end, not one. 65–80% MAS — controlled, NOT max

## 15. Band-Assisted Pull-Up

- **Video:** https://youtube.com/shorts/pZozI1iaW0k?si=khFTBdN6N3PVZMi3
- **Category:** Upper-body strength — vertical pull.
- **Main muscles:** Lats; biceps; upper back.
- **Secondary muscles:** Shoulders; grip; midline.
- **Primary cue:** Stand firmly on the band and pull your chest toward the bar.
- **Secondary cue:** Lower under control until your elbows fully straighten.
- **Equipment:** Rack OR pull-up bar, AND a resistance band.
- **Unilateral:** No; repetitions are total.
- **Experience:** Complete beginners of either gender, plus female athletes at 1–2 years only.
- **Session use:** Pull-Up regression; inherits the Pull-Up row's main-lift or secondary-lift role.
- **Availability:** Automatic regression from Pull-Ups and manual Swap from Pull-Ups only. It is not a general Add choice or a Swap from another exercise.
- **Season:** All phases; no additional game-day restriction.
- **Near-game suitability:** Good.
- **Prescription:** Inherit the Pull-Up row's sets and repetitions. Increase assistance enough to complete every prescribed repetition cleanly.
- **Loading:** Assisted bodyweight. Record band strength or colour with the existing band control and support height in the session note. Progress by gradually reducing assistance.
- **Loading demand:** Moderate.
- **Fatigue:** Moderate.
- **Soreness:** Low.
- **Balance/stabilisation demand:** Moderate.
- **Eccentric demand:** Moderate.

both sides of the band are securely supported before starting.

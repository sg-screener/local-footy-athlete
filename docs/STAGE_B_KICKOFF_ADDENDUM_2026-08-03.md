## §1 — Rulings that now BIND the engine (all Sam-signed, all gated)

1. **The Session Type Charter** (survey + rulings 2026-07-30, Bible Section
   20): RECOVERY IS DELETED as a type — never generated, never rendered
   (L-P7 deleted-vocabulary law). The generator programs SEVEN strength
   variants (Upper/Lower/Upper Pull/Upper Push/Lower Hinge/Lower Squat/
   Full). Gunshow, Mobility, Accessories are athlete-addable composed
   sessions with signed compositions (gunshow 2+2+2 from the signed pools,
   delts not shoulder-health; mobility 5-8 from the pool via the signed
   region table at warm-up doses; accessories from the prehab pools). No
   session type exists without the four answers (who places / who chooses /
   how counted / who authored contents) — the charter gate enforces it.
2. **The Optional Placement Law + 90/10:** core S&C is built FIRST by the
   contract; optional work places ONLY via the signed need-based top-up
   pass (docs/NEED_COMPUTATION_SHEET_2026-07-30.md), which runs AFTER
   requireSection18AcceptedWeek — keep that seam; it makes top-ups
   structurally unable to touch compliance. No day-based or default
   optional placement exists. G-1 gunshow (authored, fixture-relative) and
   early off-season all-optional (authored contract) are the two standing
   authored placements.
3. **THE BUILDER OWNS THE WEEK** (repair-capacity ruling): the allocator
   satisfies the contract by construction or reports which requirement it
   could not place. Repairs exist for genuine one-off misses only:
   attach-first → standalone → visibly-optional (step 3 recorded as law,
   unbuilt — build only against a real red). Stage B's assembler IS the
   question-5 collapse destination: derive the whole week from declared
   days + contract, no repair phase to starve. Free days are capacity for
   all domains; nothing reads "what occupies a day" as evidence about it.
4. **MOBILITY PAIRING** (docs/MOBILITY_PAIRING_RULINGS_2026-07-31.md —
   Sam's signature feature): on strength days, 2-3 accessories pair with
   mobility as DEFAULT supersets; main lifts never paired; non-compete rule
   (mobility region ∉ accessory's ∪ main regions, prefer session-untouched);
   warm-up doses; not logged, counts toward nothing; floor-based picks
   preferred. Session composition work — build it into the assembler.
5. **THE ROLLING TWO-BLOCK HORIZON:** the app maintains roughly two
   training blocks of visible future; later weeks derive when they enter
   the horizon, from the freshest logged reality. END STATE: horizon
   derived on demand — no stored future blocks, no rollover event. The
   interim rollover (45d717a) retires when Stage B's derivation makes it
   meaningless. This is L16's vertical slice: prove
   load → display → change → repair → approve → persist → relaunch-identical
   on the derived horizon before breadth.
6. **CONDITIONING VOCABULARY COLLAPSES TO THE 55.** The generator's third
   vocabulary ("5 x 8min zone 2 Rower", pinned in optionalTopUpTests §C)
   dies; every conditioning session an athlete sees is one of Sam's 55
   signed templates (or the signed TT). The declared red
   generated_conditioning_rows_have_no_authored_name is PAID by this stage.
7. **EQUIPMENT IS THE ATHLETE'S ANSWER:** selection reads
   resolveEquipmentCapabilities over the typed EquipmentAnswer (HAVE/NEVER
   + this-week facts). The vocabulary is derived from the library
   (equipmentVocabulary gate). Nothing infers a kit; shrink-never-pad under
   restriction (gunshow precedent).
8. **WEAK-POINT READING A** (weakPointFocus, bound to :105): a stated
   weakness changes SELECTION only — never counts, never distributions.
   Power & explosiveness is its own fifth category leaning the power pool
   and acceleration-flavoured speed work. The no-answer default order is
   gated law.
9. **TEAM-NIGHT SIZE, estimate→measured:** onboarding intensity/duration
   seed only; the rolling last-3 logged team nights (Light/Normal/Hard,
   SessionFeedback) own team-night size; a Hard night informs next WEEK.
   Team night = hard day unconditionally (:119). Stacking and adjacent-day
   decisions read the derived size.
10. **PROJECTION CONTRACT:** the engine never produces athlete-facing
    words. It emits typed facts (composedOptionalKind, fixtureVariant,
    part kinds, typed roles) that project() + SignedCopy render. One name
    per session on cards (L-P8); practice matches are fixtureVariant-typed;
    every new string arrives via the copy sheet for Sam's signature.
    Surface-agreement laws L-P1–P8 and the deep walker are acceptance
    gates for every stage.
11. **INJURY ROUTING has one owner** (data/injuryRegions.ts, LR-27
    converged): selection filters read the owner; the divergence gate
    stays armed.
12. **SHORT-ON-TIME ruled effect:** a time-capped day derives the
    compressed session — main lift kept, cut to essentials, under the
    35-minute time-cap owner. The schedule-fact deriving lane (approved

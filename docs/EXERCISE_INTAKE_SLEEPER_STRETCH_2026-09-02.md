## 13. Sleeper Stretch

- **Video:** https://youtube.com/shorts/clqjaMIRWfM?si=GbVzhQVqIB8JTLBs
- **Category:** Mobility.
- **Main muscles:** Shoulders.
- **Secondary muscles:** None supplied.
- **Primary cue:** Lie on the target shoulder and gently lower the forearm.
- **Secondary cue:** Stop at a mild stretch behind the shoulder, never the front.
- **Equipment:** No equipment.
- **Unilateral:** Yes; hold duration is per side.
- **Experience:** Everyone; use a gentle, comfortable range.
- **Session use:** Shoulder mobility; upper-body warm-up; Mobility; Recovery.
- **Availability:** Automatic in upper-body Movement Prep, Mobility and Recovery; manual Add/Swap; never automatic in Primer.
- **Season:** All phases.
- **Near-game suitability:** Caution; short gentle holds are allowed at G-1, but avoid aggressive or long holds immediately before throwing, pressing or contact.
- **Prescription:** 2 × 30 seconds per side; 15 seconds rest, using the lower bound of the supplied 15–30-second range.
- **Loading:** Bodyweight with gentle pressure from the opposite hand; no external loading and no bouncing.
- **Loading demand:** Low.
- **Fatigue:** Low.
- **Soreness:** Low.
- **Balance/stabilisation demand:** Low.
- **Eccentric demand:** Low.

**Specific painful movements / restrictions:** Lying directly on the target
shoulder; internal rotation with the arm raised; pressure through the elbow;
anterior shoulder pinching. Do not force the hand towards the floor. Recent
shoulder dislocation, surgery or known instability needs review.

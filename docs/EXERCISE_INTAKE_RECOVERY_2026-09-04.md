# Exercise intake — the 33 recovery exercises — 4 September 2026

Owner: integrate. Status: ruled by Sam, 4 September 2026 (R-364): *for these
33 exercises, the complete intake process supersedes the 24 July untagged
exemption.* "Mobility" must not mean "safe for every injury"; every exercise
carries injury behaviour based on what it physically demands.

How each row was classified, in Sam's words: from its actual movement, its
loading and its existing contraindications. Every pool contraindication Sam
authored (4 May, 25 July, 28 July) is preserved as **Avoid**, so the exercise
stays refused at selection (the pool filter, unchanged), as a swap and on an
existing session at every severity. Regions the drill loads or stretches
without a contraindication are **Caution**; the rest are **Good**. Doses,
equipment, cues, videos and muscles are the existing authored data; this
intake adds no equipment and changes no route. Classification precedents:
Horse Stance Hold (a squat-shaped hold classified isolation_lower), Sleeper
Stretch (isolation_upper), Crab Hold / Bench Thoracic Extension (core), and
the conditioning family ruling for the three walks (lower body Caution,
"severity does the rest").

Not authored here, by boundary: no new equipment or equipment filter (the
equipment sheet is untouched; the intake test reads each pool entry's own
equipment), and no tags prescription (the pool entry owns the dose, as it
always has).

## 01. Hip 90/90 Stretch

- **Category:** Mobility.
- **Main muscles:** Hips
- **Secondary muscles:** Groin

- **Secondary cue:** Stay tall, shift weight gently.
- **Equipment:** No equipment. Existing pool equipment; no new equipment vocabulary.

- **Experience:** Everyone.
- **Session use:** Hip mobility; Mobility; Recovery; Primer hip-mobility slots.
- **Availability:** Unchanged — the same automatic Mobility / Recovery / flow routes and manual Add/Swap as before this intake; no new route.
- **Season:** All phases.
- **Near-game suitability:** Good; comfortable holds are fine the day before a game.

- **Loading demand:** Low.
- **Fatigue:** Low.
- **Soreness:** Low.

- **Eccentric demand:** Low.
- **Pool contraindications (Avoid, kept on the pool entry):** hip; knee.

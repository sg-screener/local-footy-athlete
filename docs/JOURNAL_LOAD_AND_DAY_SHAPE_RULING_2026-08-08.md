## 1. "LOAD VS YOUR NORMAL" — RULED: hard-weighted session count

Sam ruled option (b): load = sessions completed, weighted by day
hardness, compared against the rolling 4-week average (the base design's
ACWR-lite, maths kept simple, language honest). His words: "yes how my
bible thinks about weeks" (Bible: the hard-days-per-week frame).

WEIGHTS SIGNED same day, Sam's own numbers: **Hard day = 2, Moderate
day = 1, Easy day = 0** — his words: "do 2 - 1 - 0 though, easy days
don't effect fatigue but count as sessions." Two consequences, both his:
easy days are INVISIBLE to the load number, and they still COUNT in
"did the work happen" session counts — load and completion are two
different questions and only load ignores easy days. Provenance law
satisfied: every constant here is Sam-authored.

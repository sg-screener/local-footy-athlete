## 16. Incline Push-Up

- **Video:** https://youtube.com/shorts/7f8JOu0i1cQ?si=5yAE7LhP43eJR7nX
- **Category:** Upper-body strength — horizontal push.
- **Main muscles:** Chest; triceps; shoulders.
- **Secondary muscles:** Midline; glutes. Serratus anterior maps to Shoulders, already represented in the main group.
- **Primary cue:** Keep a straight body line and lower your chest to the support.
- **Secondary cue:** Press away without letting your hips sag or shoulders shrug.
- **Equipment:** Bench OR plyo box.
- **Unilateral:** No; repetitions are total.
- **Experience:** Complete beginners of either gender, plus female athletes at 1–2 years only.
- **Session use:** Push-Up regression; inherits the Push-ups row's strength or accessory role.
- **Availability:** Automatic regression from Push-ups and manual Swap from Push-ups only. It is not a general Add choice or a Swap from another exercise.
- **Season:** All phases; no additional game-day restriction.
- **Near-game suitability:** Good.
- **Prescription:** Inherit the Push-ups row's sets and repetitions. Raise the hand position enough to complete every repetition cleanly.
- **Loading:** Bodyweight. A higher hand position is easier; progress by gradually lowering the support. Record support height in the existing session note, not kilograms.
- **Loading demand:** Low.
- **Fatigue:** Low.
- **Soreness:** Low.
- **Balance/stabilisation demand:** Low.
- **Eccentric demand:** Moderate.

## 18. Banded 90/90 External Rotation

- **Video:** https://youtube.com/shorts/PTi9pfttH64?si=cfQmGyB75VWotbcg
- **Category:** Accessories / prehab — Shoulder health.
- **Primary plane:** Transverse.
- **Secondary planes:** None.
- **Main muscles:** Shoulders.
- **Secondary muscles:** None supplied.
- **Primary cue:** Keep your elbow level with your shoulder and rotate your forearm upward.
- **Secondary cue:** Keep ribs down; avoid shrugging or forcing the range.
- **Equipment:** Resistance band.
- **Unilateral:** Yes; repetitions are per arm.
- **Experience:** Everyone.
- **Session use:** Shoulder health; rotator-cuff accessory; upper-body warm-up; overhead preparation.
- **Availability:** Automatic in upper-body Movement Prep and Shoulder health slots; manual Add/Swap; not automatic in Primer.
- **Season:** All phases; suitable before training and at G-1 when pain-free.
- **Prescription:** 2 × 15–20 repetitions per arm with 20 seconds rest, exactly matching Banded External Rotation.
- **Loading:** Band resistance.
- **Loading demand:** Low.
- **Fatigue:** Low.
- **Soreness:** Low.
- **Balance/stabilisation demand:** Low.
- **Eccentric demand:** Low.
- **Near-game suitability:** Good.

**Specific painful movements / restrictions:** Shoulder abduction at 90 degrees;
external rotation; holding the elbow elevated; gripping the band. Reduce band
resistance or range if the athlete shrugs, arches backwards or feels anterior
shoulder pinching. Recent shoulder dislocation, surgery or significant
instability needs review.

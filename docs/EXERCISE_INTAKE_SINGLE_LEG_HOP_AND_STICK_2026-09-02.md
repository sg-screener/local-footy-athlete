## 17. Single-Leg Hop and Stick

- **Video:** https://youtube.com/shorts/ml-8WNXFJxw?si=_oGKDJbcRA66vTqC
- **Category:** Power / plyometrics — lower-body explosive work.
- **Main muscles:** Glutes; quads; calves.
- **Secondary muscles:** Hamstrings; groin; midline; feet.
- **Primary cue:** Hop forward from one leg and freeze the landing.
- **Secondary cue:** Land quietly, absorb through the hip, and hold your balance.
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight only; no equipment.
- **Unilateral:** Yes; repetitions are per leg.
- **Experience:** 2+ years only; male and female athletes at the consistent or advanced tiers. Sam explicitly excluded the 1–2-year tier.
- **Session use:** Lower-body power.
- **Availability:** Automatic in lower-body power blocks and manual Add/Swap for 2+ Years athletes; unavailable to beginners and 1–2-year athletes.
- **Season:** All phases once basic landing control is established; suitable in-season at low volume. The existing power policy excludes G-1 automatically.
- **Near-game suitability:** Caution.
- **Prescription:** 2 sets of 5 reps per side; 120 seconds rest. Reset fully before every repetition. The app stores the lower bound of the supplied 2–3-set range, following the existing intake convention.
- **Loading:** Bodyweight only. Begin with short-to-moderate hops and progress distance while maintaining a controlled landing.
- **Loading demand:** Moderate.
- **Fatigue:** Low.
- **Soreness:** Low.
- **Balance/stabilisation demand:** High.
- **Eccentric demand:** High.
- **Impact:** High; unilateral take-off and landing.

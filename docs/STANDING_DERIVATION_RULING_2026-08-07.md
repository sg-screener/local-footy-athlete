# STANDING DERIVATION RULING — 2026-08-07

Ruled by Sam, in his own words: "yes — the ledger's link derives from
the decision, never from a stored before/after diff — why can't we
just tell it that if it's always the bug?" This doc tells it that.

## The standing rule — no stop needed

When measurement finds a stored representation feeding a computation,
the ruling is PRE-GIVEN: derive it from the decisions and facts that
produced it, and retire the stored copy. The terminal proceeds
without a seat round-trip, applying the standard discipline
(price/scaffold first, witnesses unedited, systemic not per-site,
resolved authority, one owner, boundary report records the
application of THIS doc).

## The three STOP conditions that survive

A seat ruling is still required, before building, when:

1. CLASSIFICATION IS UNCERTAIN — the stored thing might be a DECISION
   payload (athlete data: what was binned, accepted, marked, answered)
   rather than computed output. The test: could this be recomputed
   from the ledger + facts alone? If genuinely unclear after
   measurement, stop. Deleting a decision is unrecoverable; deleting
   an output never is.
2. A SAM-SIGNED BEHAVIOUR OR WORD would change (signed copy, signed
   product behaviour like LR-28's restore) — supersession needs a
   dated ruling, always.
3. THE WITNESSES DISAGREE after the derivation is priced — a residual
   red means the mechanism is not what it seemed, and today proved
   that is where the real findings live.

Everything else — where the derivation installs, which sites route,
what retires — is the terminal's to build and the boundary report's
to prove.

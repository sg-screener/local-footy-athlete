## 1. Goal, restated as acceptance criteria

The coach is "90%" when, on Sam's phone, against a real generated athlete:

1. **He answers from the athlete's real situation** — phase, block and week
   kind, this week AND next, equipment, position, goals, injuries, the lifts
   and their estimates — and never has to guess any of them from day names.
2. **He applies the current rule book** — a question whose answer is owned by a
   ruling or a Bible section gets that ruling, cited, and a ruling added on
   Monday reaches him by the next deploy with no hand step forgotten.
3. **He knows the app** — when the athlete's need has a door (Tired / Sick /
   Injured, Move, Remove, Log training, Equipment changed, Profile rows), he
   names the door by its on-screen label and where it is, in one sentence.
4. **He remembers** — the conversation survives leaving the tab for as long
   as the app is open (R-398: gone on exit, nothing stored anywhere); what the
   athlete recorded in past weeks (readiness, outcomes, changes, injuries) is
   context, derived from records the app already keeps.
5. **Every one of the above has a Coach Lab case Sam has reviewed**, and the
   coach suites sit inside the release gate.

**Non-goals (this plan):** the coach changing the program (R-136/R-138 — a
separate confirmation-path decision, see §8); a new model or provider (R-137);
rewriting the Bible or rulings into a condensed form (R-134 forbids it);
medical triage beyond R-108/R-135.

### Rock 2 — The rule book: fresh, findable, deployed

Three defects, three fixes, all inside R-134 (exact line-named excerpts, no
saved mini-Bible).

**2a — findable.** Replace 72-line sliding windows with **semantic units**:
one chunk per ruling (`## R-nnn` / `**R-nnn**` boundaries) and one per Bible
section. Raise `active_rule` cap 2 → 8 (ruling rows are short; the 60,000-char
budget still holds). Score against **the whole situation, not just the
message**: add to `queryWeights` the exercises and headlines of the day the
question names (or today), the phase/week-kind words, and the door words from
Rock 3. Add **precomputed embeddings** for every chunk at build time and rank by
keyword + cosine together (one embedding call for the query at runtime; if it
fails, keyword ranking alone — never a refusal). Add a deterministic **pinned
set**: rulings whose `phase:` tag matches the athlete's phase are always
candidates; tags are a generated index (`R-nnn → [in_season, nordic, …]`),
not a paraphrase, reviewed once and versioned with the bundle.
Test: a retrieval regression suite — for 30 (question, snapshot) pairs, the
owning ruling id must be in the selected set (Nordic-in-season → R-394 is the
first case).

**2b — fresh.** `npm run coach:deploy` = `coach:knowledge:build` →
`test:coach-chat-integration` (the freshness cell) → `supabase functions

### Rock 4 — Memory, in three layers

**L2 first, because it is free of new state:** the app already records
readiness, outcomes, changes, injuries and notes. Rock 1's
`readinessHistory`, `sessionOutcomes`, `recentChanges`, `restrictions` ARE
most of "memory" — derived, never stored twice (NORTH_STAR). Ships with 1a.

**L1 — conversation continuity (R-398, as ruled).** The turns move from
`CoachTabScreen`'s `useState` to an app-level in-memory holder (a context or a
non-persisted store slice) so they survive leaving the tab and die with the
app process. Nothing to disk, nothing to a server. `MAX_RECENT_TURNS` 6 → 10
in app and server together. The Privacy sentence "not saved" stays true.

**L3 — DROPPED by R-398** (kept here only so nobody re-proposes it without the row). Was: Facts with no
field: "burpees wreck my wrists", "knee clicks on stairs". The coach may end an
answer with one question — *"Want me to remember that?"* — the app renders it
as a card with Yes / No (system-raised, like the weekly question, R-105
shape); Yes writes `{text, dateISO}` to an athlete-visible list under My
Status, deletable there. The stored item is an athlete decision (an input),
which the north star allows. It travels in the snapshot as `athleteNotes[]`.
The response schema gains `rememberOffer: string | null` — **not** a program
action; `programActions` stays empty and gated. Journal free-text notes stay
out (opt-in + App Store label, parked in doors §9) unless Sam folds them into
the same Yes.

# PROPOSED L13 — the walker reaches ACCUMULATED state

**STATUS: PROPOSED, awaiting Sam's ratification. Endorsed by review.** Recorded
here rather than added to AGENTS.md, because a process law is not a law until Sam
says it is.

## The law

**A harness that only reaches freshly-acted worlds tests a life nobody lives.**
The walker must reach ACCUMULATED state — long athlete lives, many edits deep,
time advancing — and the athlete-facing suites must assert over those states, not
only over three-actions-from-install.

## Why it is proposed now: it has cost three units

1. **The hydration wipe.** The refusal could not be reproduced because Sam's
   2026-08-03 week breached a current rule only after **43 revisions** of edits.
   The acted sequence was onboard, generate, marks — and its weeks re-evaluate
   cleanly. The law was eventually proven by injecting the refusal, which was
   right for a law about aftermath, but the CAUSE remains unreproduced.
2. **Surface-agreement cells 1 and 4.** Both pass today. Cell 1 (three surfaces,
   three stories) and cell 4 (recovery day gets add-only) depend on a day that has
   been edited repeatedly; in a freshly-acted world the G+1 Sunday resolves a real
   session, so the menu offers everything and the surfaces agree. The defects Sam
   photographed most directly are the ones the harness cannot yet reach.
3. **The G-1 ask (closed).** The device-findings cell tapped a move off an EMPTY
   Thursday because a generated week puts nothing there; his tape recorded
   `override:set:2026-08-06`. One added session fixed it — which is the same
   lesson at depth one.

Three different units, three different layers, one shape: **the state where the
defect lives is deeper than the state the harness builds.**

## What it forbids

- Loosening an assertion so it goes red in a shallow world. Cells 1 and 4 stay as
  they are; they go red by the walker walking further, not by asking less.
- Treating "passes in a freshly-acted world" as evidence of absence. A pass at
  depth 3 says nothing about depth 43, and a report must say which depth it means.

## What it requires

- The walker's budget gains a DEPTH dimension beside its width: not only "how many
  walks" but "how long a life" — dozens of edits, weeks of advancing time, marks
  set and cleared, facts declared and resolved.
- Athlete-facing suites state the depth they reached, so a green cell can be read
  correctly.
- Where a defect is known to need depth (cells 1 and 4 today), the cell records
  the depth it needs and stays red-or-unreached rather than being quietly
  satisfied by a shallow pass.

## Relationship to existing law

L11 says the matrix comes before the phone. **L13 says the matrix must be deep
enough to contain the defect** — a matrix that enumerates every door at depth 3
still sends Sam looking for what happens at depth 40. L12 asks what catches the
next defect of a class; this names one answer that keeps recurring.

## Cost, stated honestly

Depth is expensive. The walker's bounded budget is already 10×14 and every walk
generates a program; a 40-edit life is several times that per walk. This will need
a separate budget tier — the bible runs a shallow sweep, a nightly or pre-merge
run goes deep — and that split must be declared, because a shallow gate that
LOOKS like the deep one is the failure this law exists to prevent.

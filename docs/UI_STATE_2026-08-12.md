## What the design is measured against

`docs/design/LFA_UI_PROTOTYPE_2026-08-10.html` — the signed prototype, and the
governing rule from `UI_MERGE_PLAN_2026-08-10.md:11` is unchanged: **HER
STRUCTURE, HIS STYLE.** Colour, font and icon differences from the prototype are
NOT findings. Structure differences are.

**How to check parity, reproducibly:** render the prototype headless at phone
width driving its screen radios directly (`screenProgram`+`modeDay`, `modeWeek`,
`screenCoachChat`), and put it beside the `ui-walk` shots. That method found the
week-list divergence on 2026-08-11 and confirmed its repair on 2026-08-12.

**Supersedes V2, which supersedes the first draft. Both are left as written.**

the eight rows were ruled. This is the current one and it signs standalone.

## 1. Optional work — REPLACES V2's amendment 1

V2 stated the five conditions. Sam's rulings of the same day made the mechanism
concrete, and the mechanism is the stronger statement.

**Proposed amendment:**

> **THE OPTIONAL PLACEMENT LAW.** No defaults. The app builds the program; if
> anything is lacking, it adds a spare optional session to make up for it.
> Strength and conditioning is the 90% — accessories, mobility and the rest are
> the final 10%.
>
> Optional placement is a **need-based top-up pass**: it runs AFTER the core week
> is built, computes what the week lacks, and places at most what fills the lack.
> **No day-based or default placement of optional work exists anywhere.**
>
> Whatever it places must be a REAL COMPOSED SESSION from an authored pool —
> Accessories from the prehab pools, Mobility from the mobility pool, per the
> signed structures. A sentence describing accessory work on a day is invented
> composition and is not a session.
>
> Every optional session is rendered visibly optional, is binnable in one tap,
> and is never counted toward compliance, load, or rest.
>
> **Two authored exceptions**, which are rules and not defaults:
> - **the G-1 Gunshow** — fixture-relative, `:153` "everyone may take an optional
>   Gunshow at G-1";
> - **early off-season** — `:109`, the first two weeks are optional for
>   everything.

**If you don't sign it:** the app keeps placing optional work on days rather than
on needs — which is four rows you have already killed — and keeps placing focus
strings where sessions belong.

## 2. The rest quota — AND THE BIBLE ALREADY SAID IT

**Found while drafting the need computation, and it changes what this row is.**
`:116`, in the Bible today, unamended:

> "**Rest days are days with no required S&C work.** They are used to freshen up,
> absorb training, recover from games, or create space around heavy training
> days. **Optional easy recovery is okay which involves a light mobility flow**,
> and potentially a walk or something, but rest days should not secretly become
> hard training days."

That is the Rest law, word for word, and it has been in the Bible the whole time.
The app was not missing a ruling — **it was in breach of a line you had already
written**, and `trueRestDays = !activeDays.has(day)` contradicted it directly.

**So this row needs no signature.** It is an enforcement note, not an amendment:

> The rest quota reads `:116` literally. A day carrying only optional work is a
> rest day; the week reports it as both rested and active.

The only clause worth ADDING is the second half of your law, which `:116` implies
and does not say outright:

> **Proposed addition to `:116`:** "…and optional work the athlete added never
> costs them the rest day."

**If you don't add it:** nothing breaks — the behaviour is already `:116`'s. The
sentence only closes the reading that let the app charge an athlete for their own
foam rolling.

## 4. Mobility — REVISED from V2, now with the signed detail

> **MOBILITY.** A mobility session is COMPOSED from the mobility pool: **6
> movements** (window 5-8) at their authored warm-up doses, spread across
> **lower, hips, midline and upper**. No pre-built flow sits between the pool and
> the athlete. Counts toward nothing: never a hard day, no exposure credit, never
> breaks rest.
>
> **The app plans mobility sessions in the OFF-SEASON only** — `:104`, mobility
> gains belong to the off-season and chasing them in-season or pre-season risks
> injury. In every other phase mobility is athlete-add, plus the in-session flow.

The region table and the 6-movement target are **signed** (2026-07-30) and are in
the code as signed. The `:104` clause is new to this draft — it came out of the
need-computation work and is the reason there is no in-season mobility need.
